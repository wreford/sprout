How to play

Press and swipe across adjacent hexes to trace a word. Release your finger to submit. Swipe back onto the previous hex to backtrack.

Words must be at least 3 letters and pass a live dictionary check.

Score by length:

3 letters3
4 letters5
5 letters8
6 letters13
7 letters21
8+40

Golden hex — the shimmering tile doubles the whole word when included. It moves after you use it.

Rare letters — J · Q · X · Z · K each add +3 bonus when used.

Combo — chain a word within 9 seconds to unlock ×2, then ×3, then ×4. Keep the streak alive.

Endless mode. Used tiles get replaced with fresh letters — the hive never runs out. Your best score sticks per device.
