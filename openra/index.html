OpenRA Maps

Custom Red Alert maps for OpenRA. Download, drop in your maps folder, play.

How to install a map

  1. Download the .oramap file.
  2. Put it in your Red Alert maps folder for your OpenRA version:
    • Windows: Documents\OpenRA\maps\ra\<version>\
    • Mac: ~/Library/Application Support/OpenRA/maps/ra/<version>/
    • Linux: ~/.config/openra/maps/ra/<version>/
  3. Start OpenRA, open Multiplayer or Skirmish, and pick the map. Everyone in the game downloads it automatically from the host.
