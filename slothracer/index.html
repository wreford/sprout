🦥 Sloth Racer

the world's slowest racing game

Four sloths. One branch. Speeds that scientists describe as "technically motion."

How to race: a ring breathes on screen — slowly in, slowly out. Tap BREATHE at the still point, the moment it is smallest. A well-timed breath becomes a long, smooth, unhurried pull along the branch.

Mistime it — or rush — and your sloth stops to yawn. Yawns cost more than patience ever will.

Chain calm breaths to build zen: your pulls grow longer and butterflies come to sit on you. The butterflies do not help. They are butterflies.

Your sloth always inches forward on its own. You cannot lose by relaxing — only by hurrying.

AUTO lets the sloth breathe for itself while you just watch the jungle. This is allowed. This is encouraged.
