Field kit

Three spreadsheets that open on the answer. Each comes loaded with a worked example, so you see the payoff before you type anything. They open in Excel, Google Sheets and LibreOffice.

Is It Really Done?

Progress proven by the records, next to what the last report said. Mark each item's evidence Y or N, and every item shows DONE or what it's missing.

Download (.xlsx)Goes with Done Should Be Calculated, Not Typed

Who's Holding Up the Job?

Type what each item is waiting on. Every chain is followed to the person at the end, and the dashboard tells you who to call first.

Download (.xlsx)Goes with Every Open Item Should Explain Itself

How Long Did Bad News Take?

Two dates for every problem: when someone saw it, and when the plan knew. Find the slowest stage and watch the weekly median fall.

Download (.xlsx)Goes with How Long Bad News Takes to Reach the Plan

Grey columns are typed; dark-headed columns calculate. Replace the example rows with your own. Nothing in them connects to anything: the files stay wherever you save them.
