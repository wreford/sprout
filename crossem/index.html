How to play

You get 6 words. Lay them on the board crossword-style — after the first word (which must cover the ★), every word has to cross an existing word through a shared letter. Like this:

TREE
·A·A
·TURD
···T
···H

Scoring is Scrabble-ish: letters have values, bonus squares multiply — but only when you first cover them. Order matters. Big letters on big squares = big numbers.

Double letter Triple letter Double word Triple word

Stuck? Toss a word you can't place (it scores nothing). New hand every day.
