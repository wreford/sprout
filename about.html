About

TaskIAP is a small, anonymous site about construction project management, written from Ontario. The journal is notes on how jobs run and what helps. The Bootcamp is free PMP exam practice. The game is for fun.

Nothing here is professional, legal or safety advice. Nothing you do on the site is sent anywhere: progress in the Bootcamp and the game stays in your browser.
