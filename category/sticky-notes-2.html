Is a bad attitude the person or the job?

Mostly the job. Give someone ready work, the right tools and straight answers and most of it goes away. The few who hide problems, fudge progress or cut safety corners should go quickly, because everyone is watching what you put up with.

How long should a coordination meeting be?

Long enough to get every red item to a name and a date. If that takes two hours, the meeting isn't the problem.

Sticky notes · Mar 15, 2026

Should foremen see the whole schedule?

Probably not. They need three weeks of their own work and the five things holding it up. It's also why the schedule review takes two hours: we go through five thousand activities when the crews only need the next three weeks.
