This is a cartoon. Real roads are worse and have no retry button.
  • Cross at a crosswalk or a light. Every time.
  • Stop. Look left, right, left again. Then listen.
  • Make eye contact with the driver before you step out. If you cannot see their eyes, they cannot see you.
  • A big truck needs the length of a football pitch to stop. It cannot swerve for you.
  • Trucks have enormous blind spots — right at the front, right behind, and down both sides.
  • Headphones off. Phone down. Hood back.
