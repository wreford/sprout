🔩 Kiwi's Engineering Workbench

Kiwi has opened a machine shop, and the whole neighborhood has orders.

📐 CAD — drag the control points to draft your part on real grid paper. The blueprint dimensions itself.

🌀 LATHE — press on the spinning blank to cut it down toward your blueprint. Wood only comes off, never back on — just like real life. Sandpaper smooths your sins a little.

🧪 TEST — physics runs on the actual shape you carved. Tops wobble if the tip is blunt. Necks snap if they're thin. Pins thump if they're lumpy.

design it, make it, test it. that's the whole secret of engineering
