Changes made here:
- Portraits (img/portraits/) were scaled down to 200 pixels wide and saved as WebP.
- Story backdrops (img/story/) were scaled down to 1200 pixels wide.
- Music and sounds were converted from Ogg Vorbis and WAV to MP3 so every browser can play them.
- Unit sprites are unchanged on disk; the game recolours their team-colour pixels when drawing them.
Everything else is unchanged.
