What the price buys

  • The product. A deliberately sleek single-file web app — a four-step card designer built on pure typography: 12 occasions, 6 ink colours, 3 typefaces, live previews, optional QR attachments, scheduled mailing, saved recipients, and order history with one-tap reorder and ecard re-download. No images anywhere — nothing external can break or slow it down.

Sold honestly

kardigram is pre-revenue. There are no customers, no traffic history, and no income to show you — this is a turnkey product and brand, not a cash-flowing business. Checkout is complete up to payment: the buyer wires in a processor (a one-seam Stripe drop-in), adds a lightweight order-intake endpoint for scale, and supplies the printer, card stock, and stamps the playbook specifies. The price is for the build, the brand, and the plan — nothing else is claimed.
