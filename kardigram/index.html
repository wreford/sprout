Quick questions.

How fast does a card actually arrive?

Cards ordered before 3pm ET print and get sealed the same afternoon at our London Ontario studio. They're dropped at Richmond Post the next morning and arrive in 3-5 business days anywhere in Canada, 5-7 to the US.

What does the QR on the back scan to?

Whatever you attach in the message step. A Spotify link, a YouTube video, a Google Photos album, an Instagram post, or a plain-text message you type. The QR is generated at print time and printed at 200 DPI so any phone camera can read it in one pass.

Can I schedule a card for after a specific date?

Yes. Every kardigram lets you set a mail-on date up to 12 months out. Series mode lets you schedule up to 12 cards over any interval. All cards are prepaid at order time and mailed on their scheduled dates.

Is my recipient's address safe?

Addresses live in your device's local storage — we never see them on our servers unless you place an order. Orders are encrypted in transit, addresses are auto-purged from our system 90 days after delivery, and we don't sell data. Period.

What if the card gets lost in the mail?

We reprint and re-mail on us, no receipt needed. Every kardigram has a delivery confirmation attached to the postage. If it hasn't shown up 10 business days after we mail it, tap "Reprint" in your order history and we handle the rest.

Do you ship outside Canada and the US?

Not yet. We mail from London Ontario to any Canadian or US address — postage is included in the card price, so a $9 card really is $9 no matter where in CA or US it's headed. International is on the roadmap — join the waitlist below and we'll email you when your country is added.

A small business · run by a teenager · in London Ontario

Started in a bedroom. Grown at the kitchen table.

01

The wedding thank-you that broke me.

Grandma tried to mail a thank-you card. She couldn't drive. The drugstore aisle was overwhelming. It sat on the counter for eight months. It made me cry, and then it made me curious. Why is sending real mail so hard?

02

Two minutes, from the couch.

I sketched an app on lined paper. Pick a card, write, mail. My dad — a software person — built the first version with me on our kitchen table. We printed the first batch on the same printer we use for school forms.

03

We answer every email.

Every order is packed and sealed by hand — mine, my dad's, or a friend's — in London Ontario. If a card's late, I email you personally. This isn't a big company. It's a small one that's trying really hard to be good.
