Quick questions.

How fast does a card actually arrive?

Cards ordered before 3pm ET print and get sealed the same afternoon at our Toronto studio. They're dropped at Union Post the next morning and arrive in 3-5 business days anywhere in Canada, 5-7 to the US.

What does the QR on the back scan to?

Whatever you attach in the message step. A Spotify link, a YouTube video, a Google Photos album, an Instagram post, or a plain-text message you type. The QR is generated at print time and printed at 200 DPI so any phone camera can read it in one pass.

Can I schedule a card for after a specific date?

Yes. Every kardigram lets you set a mail-on date up to 12 months out. Series mode lets you schedule up to 12 cards over any interval. All cards are prepaid at order time and mailed on their scheduled dates.

Is my recipient's address safe?

Addresses live in your device's local storage — we never see them on our servers unless you place an order. Orders are encrypted in transit, addresses are auto-purged from our system 90 days after delivery, and we don't sell data. Period.

What if the card gets lost in the mail?

We reprint and re-mail on us, no receipt needed. Every kardigram has a delivery confirmation attached to the postage. If it hasn't shown up 10 business days after we mail it, tap "Reprint" in your order history and we handle the rest.

Do you ship outside Canada and the US?

Not yet. We currently mail from Toronto to any Canadian address ($2.50) and any US address ($4.00). International is on the roadmap — join the waitlist below and we'll email you when your country is added.
