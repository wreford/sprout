Nuclear Construction Management Bootcamp

PMP exam prep and real site calls, built around a fictional 5,000 MW nuclear build. Every right answer builds the plant.

  • 363 questions across the PMP domains, plus AWP, BIM, nuclear QA, INPO and WANO, Ontario's Green Book and more
  • Mock exams, a readiness dashboard and an exam-day countdown
  • Site crises, hazard hunts with the energy wheel, and a Superintendent who doesn't hold back
  • No account. Works offline. Progress stays on your phone.

Scope Creep

An idle project management game. Start with a lemonade stand and grow it, era by era, into a galactic empire.

  • Eleven eras: food trucks, construction, megaprojects, nuclear power, the Moon, Mars, a Dyson swarm and the galaxy
  • Risk cards that reward the right response, project management techniques as upgrades, and a crew to keep happy
  • About five hours of busy play to finish. Saves on your phone.
