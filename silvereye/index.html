HOW TO PLAY

Move with the stick (push it all the way up to run). Look by dragging the screen or the right of the deck. Hold A to fire, Z to aim (a steadier, harder shot). B throws a proximity mine; C-up sets down a turret; C-left reloads; C-right swaps weapons; C-down turns you round.

Mission: take down the guards, wreck both generators, take the intel case, then get back to the lift. Being seen raises the alarm. Medals for time, aim and coming through unhurt.

Combat sim: you against simulants. 2 phones: a duel, or the mission in co-op. First to five takedowns, or the most when time runs out.

Pickups: ammo crates, armour chests (taken before health), and a heavy blaster. Shoot a mine to set it off. Turrets can be shot down.

Programs (Select): rewrite how turrets and simulants think, in Sprout, while you play.

SILVEREYE

A love letter to GoldenEye 007 (Rare, 1997) and Perfect Dark (Rare, 2000): the proximity mines, aiming down the sight, simulants to play against, and evenings round one console with friends. Thank you to everyone at Rare who made them.

It borrows their ideas and none of their work: the lab, the code and the art here are new, and no art, sound, music or level from either game is used. GoldenEye 007 and Perfect Dark belong to their owners; this is a fan tribute, not connected with them.

How SilverEye is made: a walk through its Sprout code.

Made in Sprout. Soldiers and turret by Quaternius, the blaster by Kenney, textures by Screaming Brain Studios, sounds from the NetForge asset library: all CC0. 3D by three.js.
