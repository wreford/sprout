Your Old Schedules Are Your Best Estimating Data

At closeout, the as-built dates for every activity go into the archive and nobody opens them again. They're the best estimating data a contractor has: its own crews, its own jobs, its own weather.

The next estimate starts from published rates and somebody's memory instead, which is why the same activities come in light on job after job.

  • Quantities with the dates. Planned and actual duration plus the quantity installed gives you a rate per crew-day.
  • Why the big misses happened. Weather, a blocked work front, rework. A slow activity with a known cause tells you something different from one nobody can explain.
  • Who did it. Crews that stayed together went faster, and some work hung on one person.

Jobs only compare if they share a breakdown structure. Do that, and estimates start from what your crews did last time, and ranges come from real spread instead of a guessed plus-or-minus.
