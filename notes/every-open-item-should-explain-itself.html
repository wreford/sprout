Ask "Waiting on What?" Until You Hit a Name

Ask why a task isn't done and it takes three phone calls to find out. The crew is waiting on scaffold. Scaffold is waiting on a permit. The permit is waiting on a signature from someone at a cottage with no signal.

Each person on that chain knows their link. Nobody has the whole chain written down.

So write it down. For every open item, record what it's waiting on, then what that's waiting on, until you get to a person and a date. "Them" doesn't count. When five chains end at the same person, you know whose desk to visit first (the morning question).

In the next coordination meeting, take one red item and keep asking "waiting on what?" Most meetings stop at the first answer. The useful one is usually the fourth.
