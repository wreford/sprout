Ask What You'd Do Differently on Monday

"Improve communication with the design team." "Plan procurement earlier." These show up in every lessons report and change nothing, because nobody can act on them.

Ask a sharper question: if you started this job again on Monday morning, what would you do differently that day? The answers get concrete fast. Book the utility connection in month one. Mock up the window corner. Put the owner's finish selections on the schedule.

A lesson someone can do on a Monday is a lesson someone will do.
