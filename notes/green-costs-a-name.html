Green Should Cost a Name

trust field

Anyone can turn a cell green. It costs nothing, so it drifts optimistic — a green report is often just a report nobody wanted to argue about.

Field quality solved this decades ago. The weld is good because a person with a stamp says so, and the stamp follows them for a career. Reporting never adopted its own industry's best idea.

The check is free · the signature isn’t

So sign the greens. Fewer will appear, and the ones that do will carry information — which was supposed to be the point of a report.
