Answer What-If Questions in the Meeting

"What happens if the crane is two weeks late?" On most jobs the answer is "we'll look at it and get back to you". The answer comes a week later, after the decision has already been made by default.

If the schedule is built from the conditions behind each date, a what-if is quick: change one condition and see which dates move. The scheduler can answer before the meeting ends.

When answers are quick, people ask more questions and try out options before committing to one. It works the same way as pricing risks in days.
