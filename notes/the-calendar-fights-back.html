The Calendar Fights Back

constraints scheduling

A pour needs three dry days. The crane demobilizes in October. In-water work ends when the fish come back. None of these care about logic ties.

Windows break float math. An activity showing forty days of float in front of a window that closes in forty-one has one day, and no scheduling tool on site will say so. Miss the window and lateness stops being a delay and becomes a season.

Miss the window · pay a season

So a window belongs in the model as a constraint with an expiry, not a note in someone's drawer. The ones with cliff edges belong on the board every morning, ranked by how close the cliff is.
