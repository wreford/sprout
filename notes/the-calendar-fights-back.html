Forty Days of Float, One Day Left

A pour needs three dry days. The crane leaves in October. In-water work stops when the fish come back. None of those are logic ties, and most schedules don't model them.

Forty days of float in front of a window that closes in forty-one days is one day of float. The software won't tell you. Miss the window and you lose a season, not a week. Put the closing dates on the board and look at them every morning.
