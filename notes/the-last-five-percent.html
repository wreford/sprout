The Last 5 Percent Takes a Third of the Job

Deficiency lists, commissioning, turnover documents and closeout routinely eat a third of the calendar. They're planned as the last 5 percent, and nobody manages them until the crews are leaving.

By then the people who knew the systems are on another job, the records are scattered, and every open item depends on someone who has already demobilized.

  • Open a turnover package for each system on day one, with an owner, and fill it as the work gets done.
  • Close deficiencies as they come up, each with a photo or short clip, instead of finding 1,400 on the final walk.
  • Schedule commissioning as real work with durations and logic, not a diamond at the end.
  • Don't call 95 percent nearly done. The slowest third of the job hides behind it.

Pull the as-built schedules from your last few jobs and see how long closeout took. The owner's final opinion of you gets formed in this stretch, and so does the release of your holdback.
