Baselines Rot

scheduling metrics

The baseline was our best belief on sanction day. Two winters and forty change orders later we still measure the job against it and call the growing gap variance.

Rebaselining is treated as an admission of failure, so it happens late and politically, and the reports mean less every month in between.

If the model derived fresh from live constraints, a baseline becomes one more saved forecast to score ourselves against — kept for honesty, no longer steering. I might be underrating the discipline a frozen baseline enforces. Might.
