Put the Owner's Decisions on the Schedule

Owner decisions hold up work as surely as missing steel: finishes selections, a change approval, a tie-in date, a design question. They rarely appear on the schedule, so they never look late.

Put each one in as an activity with a need-by date, worked back from the work it holds up. Then the owner sees the same thing you do: decide by the 12th, or the drywall crew stops on the 20th.

It turns "waiting on the owner" from a complaint into a date, and owners respond well to dates.
