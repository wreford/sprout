The Two-Hour Wall

The fire code calls for a two-hour wall. That requirement gets copied into the design basis, a wall type, a firestop detail, a QC record and a commissioning check. Then the job closes out and the copies scatter.

Ten years later an electrician cores a tray through it with no idea it's rated. Keep the requirement as one record that design, construction and operations all point to, so the person holding the drill can find out why the wall is there.
