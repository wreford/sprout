Turnover Is a Photocopy

theory software

A requirement is born a database row: testable, traceable, owned. Design flattens it into drawings, construction into PDFs, turnover into boxes of binders. Four translations, no receipts.

Every handoff is lossy

Every handoff is lossy compression, and operations pays to reconstruct what the project once knew. A requirement lives from sanction to decommissioning. Hand over the database, not a photograph of it.
