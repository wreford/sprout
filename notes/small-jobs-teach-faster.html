Small Jobs Teach Faster

A three-year project gives a project manager one chance to learn from the whole cycle. A three-month job gives four a year.

That's why people who ran small jobs early often make better big-job managers: they've seen estimate, start-up, closeout and handover many times, and made the mistakes cheaply.

If you're developing people, rotate them through short jobs before the big one. And on a long job, create short loops: phase-end lessons, weekly plan reviews, small mockups.
