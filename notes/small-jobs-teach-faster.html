Small Jobs Train Project Managers Faster

A three-year project gives a project manager one full cycle to learn from. Three-month jobs give them four cycles a year.

People who ran a lot of small jobs early on have been through estimating, start-up, closeout and handover many times, and made their mistakes when they were cheap.

If you're training people, give them short jobs before the big one. On a long job, build in short feedback points: lessons sessions at the end of each phase, weekly plan reviews, small mockups.
