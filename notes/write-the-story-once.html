Write the Story Once

field software

Every foreman writes the story every day: night pour ran long, pump swap at 03:10, watch form 12-B. Then five systems ask for the same facts again — schedule update, timesheet, RFI, QC record, claim file.

The narrative is the primary record; everything else is a projection of it. Software should read the story and suggest the entries, and a person should confirm them. Suggest, never auto-file.

I keep sketching this and it keeps looking possible. The hard part isn't the parsing — it's keeping the foreman's ten minutes at ten minutes.
