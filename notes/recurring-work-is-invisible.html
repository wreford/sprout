The 200-Day Bar

scheduling theory

Scaffold inspections, permit renewals, dewatering checks — every Tuesday, for a year. CPM has no verb for "every Tuesday," so the scheduler draws one 200-day bar. The bar says nothing.

A rule that mints constraints

A recurrence is a rule that mints constraints: this week's inspection blocks this week's work, not October's. Model the rule; derive the instances. Two hundred facts, each missable on its own day.
