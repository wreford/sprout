Sticky³

field theory

The sticky-note wall is the most successful scheduling interface ever shipped: zero training, anyone can move one, the room does the planning. Its one flaw is amnesia — the wall forgets by Friday.

Same square · three questions

Sticky, cubed: the same square plus three questions — what does it block, who owns it, when does it expire. Keep the tactility, add the memory. And if the digital sticky is harder to move than paper, the wall wins, as it should.
