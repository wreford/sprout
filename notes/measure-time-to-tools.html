Measure Time to Tools

The shift starts at 7:00. The first tool touches the work at 7:50. In between: the toolbox talk, a permit that isn't signed yet, a hunt for the right fittings, a wait for the hoist.

Fifty minutes across a crew of twelve is ten hours a day. That's more than a full person-week lost every week, and no report shows it, because everyone was on site and busy.

Walk one crew for one morning with a watch. Note when tools first touch work and what they waited on. Most of it can be fixed the afternoon before: permit signed, material staged, hoist booked. The foreman's last hour sets up the crew's first one, so protect it.
