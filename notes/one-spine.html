One Spine

cost theory

The estimate has a breakdown. The schedule has one. Cost control has one. The work packages have one. They started identical, and by month six there are four dialects and a standing reconciliation meeting.

Each group copied it for a reason: they need different shapes. Cost rolls up by contract, the field by area, the schedule by system. Those are views — the mistake is materializing views into owned copies.

One breakdown. Every rollup is a query against it. Copies drift; references don't.

Ribs argue · the spine decides

Keep one spine and the meeting where three people argue over which number is real dissolves — there is one number, wearing three costumes. Reconciliation was never work; it was rent on the copies.
