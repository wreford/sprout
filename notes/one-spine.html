One Spine

The estimate has a breakdown. The schedule has one. Cost control has one. The work packages have one. They started identical, and by month six there are four dialects and a standing reconciliation meeting.

One breakdown. Everything else points at it. Copies drift; references don't.

Reconciliation produces nothing the owner would pay for; it exists because we keep copies. Whether every discipline can share one spine without strangling each other, I don't know. But the meeting where three people argue about which number is real — that meeting is the tax notice.
