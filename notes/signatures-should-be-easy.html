Signatures Should Be Easy

trust software

Today's hold-point signature is expensive in logistics — drive to site, find the binder, wet ink — and the friction teaches people to skip or batch them. Expensive to give, cheap in meaning. Backwards.

Cheap to give · heavy to mean

Signing should take five seconds on a phone, with the weight in what it means: identity attached, forever. Make the logistics free and the accountability total.
