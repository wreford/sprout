Look Further Ahead Than Your Longest Lead Time

A three-week lookahead can't catch a constraint that takes four weeks to clear. Permits, engineered supports, crane bookings, outage windows: if clearing one takes longer than the window, it's already late by the time anyone sees it.

Set the window by the slowest constraint you routinely clear, not by habit. Or run two: three weeks for the crew, and a longer one that only tracks the long-lead items and the closing windows.
