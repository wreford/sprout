Make the Lookahead Longer Than Your Longest Lead Time

A three-week lookahead won't catch something that takes four weeks to clear. Permits, engineered supports, crane bookings, shutdowns: if clearing it takes longer than the lookahead, it's already late when it shows up.

Set the lookahead by the longest thing you regularly have to clear, not by habit. Or run two: three weeks for the crews, and a longer one that only tracks long-lead constraints and closing windows.
