The Only Qualified Welder Is on Vacation

field scheduling

The schedule says 2 × WELDER, CLASS B. The job needs Dave — the only current qual for that alloy — and Dave is at his daughter's wedding until Tuesday.

The resource histogram shows welders at sixty percent utilization all month. True, and useless. Aggregates hide singletons, and singletons stop jobs.

Roles on paper · people on jobs

Names where names matter: quals, signature authority, the one operator rated for the tandem lift. Model each as what it is — a constraint with a calendar — and the wedding shows up in the plan back in March, when it costs nothing.
