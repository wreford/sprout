The Only Qualified Welder Is on Vacation

resources people scheduling

The schedule says 2 × WELDER, CLASS B. The job needs Dave, because Dave holds the only current qual for that alloy, and Dave is at his daughter's wedding until Tuesday.

Every resource-loaded schedule I've seen models roles. Almost every stoppage I've seen was a person — a qual, a vacation, a delegation nobody wrote down.

Maybe resources should carry names where names matter — quals, singletons, signature authority — and roles everywhere else. The tooling hates this. The field already works this way.
