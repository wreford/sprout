Hold the Lessons Session Before Everyone Leaves

The usual lessons-learned meeting happens at closeout, when the superintendent who lived through the foundations has been on another job for a year and the foreman who solved the tie-in has retired.

Hold a short one at the end of each phase instead: foundations, structure, envelope, systems, commissioning. Thirty minutes, the people who did the work, three questions: what surprised us, what would we do differently on Monday, and which document changes.

Ten small sessions catch more than one big one, and the memories are still fresh.
