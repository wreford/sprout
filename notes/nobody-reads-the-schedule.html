Nobody Reads the Schedule

scheduling field

The integrated schedule has five thousand activities. The people building the job run on a whiteboard, three weeks deep, updated at 06:00.

The whiteboard wins because it answers this week's question and fits on a wall. The schedule answers a contract question and fits in a plotter.

The fix probably isn't a bigger monitor. It's making the wall view and the contract view projections of the same constraint set, so the whiteboard stops being a fork of the plan.
