Can You Undo It?

Safety learned to aim at what can kill people, not at what happens most often. Project risk can steal that.

Risk matrices score probability and severity. Add a third question: can you undo it? A cost overrun that builds over three years gives you a hundred chances to step in. A contract clause signed in an afternoon may give you none. If a loss is severe and permanent, plan as if it's going to happen, and put a senior person on the decision.
