Give Suppliers Real Dates

Suppliers can tell when your must-have date has two weeks of padding in it. Pad it and they stop telling you about problems in their shop until it's too late to work around them.

Give them the real install date and tell them where you have slack. Call them when a detail looks wrong, before it turns into six RFIs. Have the laydown area clear when the truck shows up.

When something goes wrong, a supplier who trusts you will help you find a way. One you've treated badly will point you to the contract.
