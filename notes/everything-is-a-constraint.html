Everything Is a Constraint

A predecessor, a missing spool, an unsigned permit, a crane booked elsewhere, wind over the lift limit, a room too crowded to work in, a dose budget nearly spent. Seven systems manage them. They're all the same thing: something that has to be true before work can start.

CPM made logic ties the main constraint because that's what 1960s computers could handle. Ask a superintendent what stopped work this week and it's rarely a predecessor.

Dates come out the other end

If everything is a constraint, the schedule is what the constraints produce. Each one has the same fields: what it blocks, what clears it, who owns it, when it expires. A permit and a weather window differ only in how they get cleared.

Keep them in one place and you can ask each morning which ones to clear to free the most work. I don't know yet whether one system can hold all seven kinds well. I do know what it costs to keep them in seven.
