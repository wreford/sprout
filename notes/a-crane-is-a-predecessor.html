The Crane Is a Predecessor

Two lifts that need the same crane can't happen at the same time. That's as hard a rule as rebar before concrete. Most schedules keep logic in one place and resources in another, and nobody reconciles them.

So both lifts show on Tuesday, and the crane operator picks which one waits.

Draw shared capacity as a link: these two need the same crane, the same welder, the same room, the same outage. Then the sequence on paper is the sequence the site will run. It also explains why the logic shows eight days of float that never shows up on site. The logic had eight days. The crane didn't.

On the next three-week lookahead, list every crane, hoist, specialist and shutdown, and draw a line between activities that share one. The clashes jump out.
