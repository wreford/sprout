Assumed True Until Checked

constraints trust

Every plan rests on assumptions — ground conditions, vendor data, the permit will renew. They get logged once at sanction and reread rarely.

An assumption is a constraint somebody decided not to verify yet. When it fails it stops work like any other constraint; it just fails without warning.

Unwatched · not untrue yet

So assumptions probably belong in the same set as everything else, each with an owner and a recheck date. The standalone register is where they go to be forgotten.
