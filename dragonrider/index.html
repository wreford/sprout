🐉 Dragon Rider

Your dragon circles a dying ring of land. Claim 3 sunfire relics, then dive into the Necropolis and burn the Wraith King — before the rot swallows everything.

Each turn:

  • 1 · Roll and fly that many spaces.
  • 2 · Your landing space happens to you.
  • 3 · The rot rolls its own die and claims a space.
  • 💎Relic cache — beat its Guardian for a relic (+1 Fury)
  • 💀Wraith ground — a fight (win: scavenge +1 heart)
  • 🏰Village — heal 2
  • 🗼Roost — heal 3
  • 🌤️Open sky — a breather

Rotted spaces glow green — landing there is always a fight, and winning cleanses them.

Fights: your die + Fury vs their die + their power. Loser loses the difference. Ties clash again. When the rot holds 6+, every foe gets +1.

You lose at 0 hearts, or when the rot claims 12 spaces.

Just dice and tokens — it plays identically on a real table.
