🐉 Dragon Rider

Claim 3 sunfire relics, then dive into the Necropolis and burn the Wraith King — before the rot swallows the ring.

The twist — every turn you choose:

Roll two dice. Fly one of them… and the rot creeps the other, counted ahead of where you land. Both outcomes are shown before you pick. Choose the lesser evil.

  • 💎Cache — beat its Guardian for a relic (+1 Fury)
  • 💀Barrow — a fight (win: scavenge +1 heart)
  • 🏰Village — heal 2 · 🗼Roost — heal 3
  • ☁️Open sky — a breather

Rotted ground is always a fight; winning cleanses it.

Fights: your die + Fury vs their die + power. Loser loses the difference; ties clash again. Before each clash you may breathe fire: spend 1 heart for +2. When rot holds 6+, foes get +1.

You lose at 0 hearts, or 12 rotted spaces.

Every 3rd turn an omen blows in — tailwinds, war drums, a merchant balloon with a bargain…

Two dice, tokens, and nerve — still plays on a real table.
