Models, and which way they face

Every model in vendor/models, drawn with model [name, x, 0, z, 0, 1.4, clip, time]: angle 0, which in Sprout means facing along +x. The camera stands on +x looking back, so a model the right way round faces you. If one ever shows its back, its file faces the other way and the game should turn it by pi (as SilverEye does for the turret).

All CC0: soldiers, the robot, the scientist (worker), the Warden (mech), the turret, the chest, crates and generators by Quaternius; the blasters by Kenney. Credits in vendor/models/README.md.
