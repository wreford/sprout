# SPROUT·256: the future, as 1995 drew it

The console now has a third tier: `Eight.start({ bits: 256 })` gives a 480 by 270 screen in true colour where everything bright glows, with faint scanlines, sprites that turn and zoom, and four buttons in an arc, as on the big arcade machines. Smoky purple plastic over its circuits, a holographic sticker and a boot screen with a jingle. [Starlane 2025](/starlane/) is its first game: surf a glowing lane into the sunset, boosting, jumping, spinning and bombing with all four buttons.
