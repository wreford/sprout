How to squeeze

PRESS AND HOLD on a blemish. A gold ring fills as the pressure builds.

LET GO IN THE GOLD BAND — that is a clean pop. Too early does nothing. Too late and it goes everywhere, including the lens.

BLACKHEADS don't squeeze. Press and drag them out.

CYSTS are stubborn. Hold and rock side to side until they loosen, then squeeze.

CRAQUELURE — the fine cracks in old varnish — is not a blemish. Squeeze one and you have damaged a priceless painting. The critic will hear about it.
