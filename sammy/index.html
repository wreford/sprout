🐱 Sammy Tycoon

a derpy orange kitten's business empire

This is Sammy. He is orange, he is derpy, and he has decided to become a tycoon. Nobody can stop him. Nobody has tried very hard.

Boop Sammy (tap him) to earn treats. Sometimes a boop lands as a MEGA DERP — worth ×10, plus emotional damage to no one.

Press and hold to pet him. He purrs. Purring is billable.

Spend treats on ventures — from the Yarn Untangling Service (he makes it worse; people pay anyway) to Sammy Aerospace (he saw a bird; it was on the moon). Ventures earn treats even while you're away.

Watch for the red dot, wobbling vases, and sudden ZOOMIES (all production ×3 — hold on to something).

When the empire is big enough, Sammy can Take a Nap — everything resets, but he dreams up permanent Dream Yarn (+10% forever, each).

Your empire saves itself, earns while you're away, and can be exported to a file (💾 Save Keeper, bottom of the shop) to move between devices.

There are 50 hidden secrets — odd boops, strange taps, unlikely feats. Each one found makes the whole empire earn +1% forever. Hats, room upgrades, a daily gift and golden yarn unlock as the empire grows.
